Do this week's posts. Follow the section "/post-week: the whole week" of the post-desk skill exactly.

Show the week as a table first. Then take the posts one at a time: notes, captions, one question, wait. A yes for one post is not a yes for the next. Schedule only the posts the person approved, with a date and a time.

If a date is given below, do the week that holds that date.

The skill is at `~/.agents/skills/post-desk/SKILL.md`. Read it first.
